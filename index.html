Who We Are

About EDU-CARE, Guwahati

EDU-CARE operates as a private coaching institution, fostering a supportive environment to assist numerous students on their educational journey. Our primary goal is to empower individuals to conquer their apprehension of mathematical challenges by providing essential skills and encouraging the development and implementation of effective learning strategies.

Through our guidance, students develop the ability to excel in their chosen fields and make meaningful contributions to the nation's economic prosperity. At EDU-CARE, we specialize in coaching Mathematics and Statistics up to the graduate level. Additionally, we offer guidance in science subjects, including physics, chemistry, and biology, up to the 10th standard level.

✔
Foundation Mentor (Class 4 - 8): Expert guidance in Mathematics and Science to stimulate curiosity and strong conceptual core knowledge early.
✔
Senior Academic Lead (Class 9 - 12 & Degree): High school board excellence (CBSE, SEBA, AHSEC) in Mathematics & Sciences, and university graduation-level pure & applied Mathematics.
✔
Personalized Doubt-Solving: Structured weekly batches, board exam mock tests, and rigorous revision tracking.
